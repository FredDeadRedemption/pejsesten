# Gunslinger (black)

Hero: **Clown Snake**, 30 hp. 25 cards, 17 distinct.

The Quickdraw deck. A Quickdraw ability only fires on a card played the same turn it
reached your hand, so every card here either is one, pays one off, or puts a card in your
hand to arm one. Tempo is the plan: a body or a shot every turn from one, and a counter
quietly climbing behind it.

## How it plays

Five things put a card in your hand on your own turn, and each one arms a Quickdraw: the
turn draw, a trade, Hat Trick, Bottom Deal, and Fox Courier. Trading is the cheap one - 1 mana
turns a dead card into a fresh one you can fire the same turn - and three Quick Shots, three
Knife Throwers, two Fat Rats and a Bear are all Tradeable, so the deck nearly always has
something to pitch.

The counter is the real resource. Gazelle gets cheaper as it climbs, and The
Dentist cashes the whole thing in at seven mana: every Quickdraw you landed all game fires
again, targets rolled because you never picked them. Six landed shots make him a game ender;
two make him a 7/7.

Gunsmith and Gunslinger are what turn one shot a turn into a clock. Gunsmith is a 1-drop
that ends up bigger than anything you paid 4 for; Gunslinger turns every Quickdraw into a
board-wide ping, which is how the deck beats a wide board without a sweeper.

A hand full of Quickdraw cards is a bad hand. You want one in hand and one arriving.

## Keeps

Keep Quick Shot, Gunsmith, Knife Thrower and Dust Jackal. Keep Hat Trick with any one-drop.
Throw back Gazelle, Bear and The Dentist. Rat Cage is fine to keep against a fast deck.

## Tuning notes

- The deck has no unconditional removal. Quick Shot and Knife Thrower both need an arrival,
  so a resolved 4/5 on a turn you drew wrong just sits there. A plain 2-mana deal 3 is the
  first card to print if it keeps losing that way.
- The Dentist replaying *everything* is the one line above the power target. A cap of three
  would bring him in line; he is printed uncapped because that is how he was designed.
- Eight one-drops is deliberate. A trade costs 1, so the deck wants a spare mana most turns.
- Nine Beasts is flavour, not a plan. Fox Courier is the only payoff and that is on purpose:
  a real beast package would compete with the Quickdraw plan for the same 25 slots.
- Toad pulls from every omen in the pool, white and green included. Keep that pool small and
  good or it turns into a random-value card instead of a cheap body with a tail.
