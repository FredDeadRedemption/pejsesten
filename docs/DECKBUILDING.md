# Deckbuilding

- 25 cards per deck.
- Up to 3 copies of any card.
- One hero, picked when the deck is built and never drawn.

Smaller decks with 3-ofs are the main anti-RnG lever: a specific card shows up by turn 7 about 65% of the time, against 42% for a 30 card deck with 2-ofs. The plan you built is the plan you draw, so losses land on decisions instead of the shuffle.

Watch for: at 3 copies a legal deck is only 9 distinct cards, so the metagame narrows faster. Drop to 2 copies if it gets stale.

Deck size also carries the Crucible roster and the Escalate and Triad payoffs: drawing your second and third copy has to be normal for those to work. See [GAMEMODES.md](GAMEMODES.md) and [CONCEPTS.md](CONCEPTS.md).

Status: the builder caps a deck at 50 cards (`MAX_DECK_CARDS`) and does not limit copies yet, so the 25 and 3 above are the rule, not the code.

The decks that exist today are written up in `deck-dawnguard.md`, `deck-gunslinger.md` and `deck-kennel.md`.
